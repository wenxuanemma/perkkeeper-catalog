# PerkKeeper Privacy Policy

_Last updated: October 9, 2026_

PerkKeeper is a personal app for tracking credit card benefits within a family. We do not collect, sell or share your personal data.

**What the app stores, and where**
- Cards, credits, offers, spending entries, family member names and saved places are stored on your device.
- If you turn on family sharing, this data is also stored in your iCloud account (Apple CloudKit) and shared only with the family members you invite. The developer cannot access it.
- We do not ask for card numbers, bank logins or passwords. The optional "last 4 digits" field never leaves your device or your family's iCloud.

**Location**
- Optional. If you allow it, your location is used on your device to show reminders when you arrive at a saved place or a business. Location is not stored or sent anywhere. Saved places you create are synced through iCloud only if family sharing is on.

**Photos**
- Optional. Screenshots you pick for offer import are read on your device and are not stored or uploaded.

**Network requests**
- The app downloads a public list of card benefits from GitHub. This request contains no personal data; GitHub may log your IP address as with any website.

**Analytics and tracking**
- None. No ads, no analytics, no third-party SDKs, no tracking.

**Notifications**
- Reminders are scheduled locally on your device.

**Deleting your data**
- Delete the app to remove local data. To remove shared data, stop sharing in Settings → Invite or manage family (owner), or Leave family (member); you can also manage iCloud data in iOS Settings → [your name] → iCloud.
